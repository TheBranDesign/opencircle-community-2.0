Our community

Desires and Pleasures DP welcomes open-minded adults and values a space where people can connect with dignity.

Our promise

Love. Respect. Inclusion.

A positive community begins with communication, mutual respect, and clear boundaries.

Community guidelines

♡
Consent comes first

Ask, listen, and respect every yes, no, and change of mind.

◈
Respect every identity

Use the names and language people ask you to use. No harassment or discrimination.

▣
Protect privacy

Do not share another person’s identity, images, or personal details without explicit permission.

✦
Keep it welcoming

No pressure, unwanted contact, intimidation, or judgment.

This community is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over.
